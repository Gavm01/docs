## Open Broadcasts

In the sidebar, open **Broadcasts**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/telephony/outbound](https://agentclara.app/telephony/outbound).

The same screen also opens from [https://agentclara.app/launch/outbound](https://agentclara.app/launch/outbound).

The heading is **Broadcasts**. The line under it is **AI-powered outreach, follow-ups, and campaigns — all from one place.**

You need an **Active Agent** selected.

## The tabs

- **Campaigns** — outbound campaigns. Click **New Campaign** when you are ready. See [Creating campaigns](/outbound/campaigns).
- **Bookings** — booking activity from calls, including a **Booking Health (24h)** count of created, cancelled, rescheduled, deduped, and blocked bookings.
- **Templates** — **Smart Outbound Templates**. The page says: pick a ready-made playbook and Clara will handle the calling. See [Call templates](/outbound/templates).
- **Settings** — settings for this broadcasts area.

## What this is not

One-to-one replies stay in the **Inbox**.

Automatic messages after a call are [Follow-ups](/teach-clara/follow-ups).

New leads from another tool use [Lead webhooks](/outbound/lead-webhooks).
