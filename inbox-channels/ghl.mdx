## What this connection does

GoHighLevel syncs contacts and appointments so Clara stays in line with your pipeline.

It can sync **Contacts**, **Opportunities**, **Appointments**, and **Tags**. It works with the **Inbox**, **Contacts**, and appointments.

## Connect it

Open **Integrations** and choose GoHighLevel. Then:

<Steps>

<Step title="Copy the Location ID">
Log in to GoHighLevel at [https://app.gohighlevel.com](https://app.gohighlevel.com).

Go to **Settings**, then **Business Profile**.

Copy your **Location ID** from the URL. It is the text after `/location/`.

In Clara, the field is **Location ID**. The hint says you can also find it under **Settings**, then **Business Info**, then **Location ID**.
</Step>

<Step title="Create an API key">
In GoHighLevel, go to **Settings**, then **API Keys**.

Create an API key and copy it.
</Step>

<Step title="Paste both and connect">
Paste the **Location ID** and the API key into Clara, then click **Connect**.
</Step>

</Steps>

There is also an optional **Calendar ID** field.

When it works, the hub can show the connection as verified. **Sync started** means a sync is running. **Sync failed** means try again from the same card.
