## Open Webhooks

Go to [https://agentclara.app/settings/advanced/webhooks](https://agentclara.app/settings/advanced/webhooks).

The heading is **Webhooks**. The line under it is **Set up webhook endpoints and integrations**.

The page also says **Webhooks let external tools (Stripe, Meta, CRMs) send events into Clara in real time.**

A warning on the page is titled **Handle with care**. The line under it is **Misconfigured endpoints can break automations and integrations. Test changes carefully before going live.**

You need permission. If you do not have it, you see **You don't have permission to view webhooks.** or **You don't have permission to manage webhooks.**

## Lead webhooks

**Lead Webhooks** is the URL for new leads from an outside system.

If you have no URL yet, click **Generate Webhook URL**. You should see **Webhook URL generated**. Then click **Copy URL**.

The row shows **Status**, **Source**, **Last received**, and **Created**.

Full steps are in [Lead webhooks](/outbound/lead-webhooks).

## Other integrations

**Configured Webhook Integrations** says **Each integration has a dedicated endpoint on your backend.**

The table columns are **Integration**, **Endpoint**, **Status**, **Last Delivery**, and **Actions**.

If nothing is set up, you see **No webhook integrations are configured yet.**

Above the table, **Webhook Health Overview** says **See the current status of your webhook connections at a glance.** It shows **Connected Integrations**, **Failed Events (recent)**, and **Events Received (24h)**.

## Connect a provider

The page's own steps are under **How to Connect**:

1. **Create a webhook in your provider**. The line under it is **Stripe, Twilio, Meta, HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.**
2. **Use the endpoint URL from the table**. The line under it is **Copy the exact URL for your integration.**
3. **Add your signing secret**. The line under it is **Set it up in your backend so signatures can be verified.**

The page also says **Keep these URLs secret. Anyone with the endpoint can try to send events.**

## If events fail

Open **Troubleshooting**. The page lists:

- **Check your provider's webhook logs for recent errors.**
- **Confirm the endpoint URL matches exactly (no extra spaces).**
- **Verify the signing secret in your backend is correct.**
- **Make sure your backend endpoint is publicly reachable (no VPN/firewall blocking).**
- **Check the Audit Logs page for security-related events.**

Then **Open Support & Debug**.

A test that fails shows **Test ping failed**.
