## Where to connect

Open **Integrations**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/integrations/socials-apps](https://agentclara.app/integrations/socials-apps).

The hub can walk you through a **Quick Connect Wizard**:

1. **Page 1: Choose CRM** — **Select HubSpot, Salesforce, or GHL**
2. **Page 2: Connect Communications** — **Set up Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp**
3. **Page 3: Connect Scheduling & Payments** — **Add Calendly for bookings and Stripe for payments**

You can click **Skip for Now**, or **Start Wizard**. You can also connect one channel on its own.

You need permission to change integrations.

## Instagram

Instagram uses a Facebook login. When it works, you see **Instagram connected successfully!**

If you cancel the Facebook window, you see **Instagram authorization was denied** or **OAuth was cancelled or failed**.

Start the connect flow again from the Instagram card. If Facebook will not load, refresh the page and allow popups.

## Facebook

Facebook is on the same communications step as Instagram and WhatsApp. Connect it from the same hub.

## After they are connected

Messages show in the **Inbox**. Filter by **Instagram** or **Facebook**.

On the agent, a channel shows **Connected** or **Not connected**. WhatsApp can show the phone number. Instagram can show the username.
