## What white-label changes

Clients log in to a portal with your agency name on it. You set the logo, the brand color, the web address, the login line, and the name on emails you send from that portal.

Open **Agency → Settings**. Only the agency owner can open this page. Everyone else sees **You don't have access to agency settings.**

The page is **Agency Settings**. The line under the title is **Manage your agency configuration, branding, settings, and integrations.**

There are five tabs: **Profile**, **Billing**, **Client Defaults**, **White-Label**, and **Danger Zone**.

## Profile

The **Profile** tab is **Profile Settings**.

<Steps>

<Step title="Agency display name">
This is the name clients see. The box says **Agency display name**. Example: Acme Marketing Agency.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact email">
**Contact email** is how clients reach your agency.
</Step>

<Step title="Support phone">
**Support phone (optional)**. The hint under the box is **Default: UK format**.
</Step>

<Step title="Timezone">
Set **Timezone** so dates and sending times match your agency.
</Step>

<Step title="Brand primary color">
**Brand primary color** is a color picker plus a hex value, such as `#4F46E5`.
</Step>

<Step title="Agency logo">
Under **Agency logo**, drop an image or click to upload.

The box says **PNG or JPG, max 2MB** and **Recommended: 400x400 square**. If the file is the wrong type, you will see **Only PNG and JPG files are supported**. If it is too big, you will see **File too large. Max 2MB.** If the picture is too small or not a square, you will see **For best results, use a 400x400 square image.**

You can **Remove logo** later.
</Step>

</Steps>

**Email signature preview** shows a sample **Book appointment** button and the line **Powered by AgentClara**. Click **Save** when the preview looks right. You should see **Saved successfully**.

If the page says **Your agency is not yet provisioned. Contact support.**, email support@agentclara.ai before you keep going.

## White-label address

Open the **White-Label** tab.

### Subdomain

The heading is **White-label subdomain**. Clients can use an address like `yourname.agentclara.app`.

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose a name">
If you have no subdomain yet, the label is **Claim your subdomain**. Type a name such as `growthagency`. The screen adds `.agentclara.app`.

If the name is too short, you see **Must be at least 3 characters**. If it is too long, you see **Must be 30 characters or fewer**. For any other name the box will not take, you see **Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only**. A hyphen at the start or the end gets that same message.

The box checks availability as you type. **Checking...** means wait. When the name is free, that line turns green.
</Step>

<Step title="Claim it">
Click **Claim subdomain**. Confirm **Claim this subdomain?**

The confirm box says **Your subdomain will be** the name you typed, plus `.agentclara.app`. Then **You can change this later (no waiting on the first claim).** Click **Claim**.
</Step>

</Steps>

**Current subdomain** shows the live address, such as `growthagency.agentclara.app`.

To change it, use **Change subdomain** and confirm **Change your subdomain?**

The confirm box says **Your new subdomain will be** the new name, plus `.agentclara.app`. Then **Your old subdomain will redirect to the new one for 30 days, then stop working. You won't be able to change again for 30 days.**

While that wait is on, the page says **You can change this in** the number of days left.

### Your own domain

Further down, **Custom domain** lets clients use an address you already own, such as `portal.youragency.com`.

<Steps>

<Step title="Add the domain">
Under **Add a custom domain**, type the domain and click **Add domain**.

You should see **Domain added. Follow the DNS instructions below.** If it is taken, reserved, or typed wrong, the message under the box says so.
</Step>

<Step title="Prove you own it">
While the status is waiting on DNS, add the TXT record the page shows.

- **Type** is TXT
- **Host** looks like `_vercel.portal.youragency.com`
- **Value** is the token on the page

Some DNS providers want the host without your domain, so you enter `_vercel` instead of `_vercel.portal.youragency.com`. Use the format your provider asks for.
</Step>

<Step title="Point the domain">
After ownership is verified, the page asks for a CNAME record.

- **Type** is CNAME
- **Name** is your domain
- **Value** is the target shown on the page (`cname.vercel-dns.com.`)

Then you will see **DNS is configured. We're setting up your SSL certificate. This usually takes 1–5 minutes.**

When it is live, the domain is linked. Open it from the page to check.
</Step>

</Steps>

Click **Refresh status** if it seems stuck. If setup fails, the page shows the error. Click **Remove domain** and add it again.

Click **Remove domain** and the page asks **Remove this domain? You will need to re-verify DNS records to use it again.**

If you changed domains recently, you may have to wait before adding another. The error tells you when the wait ends.

### Email and the login screen

On the same **White-Label** tab, **Email & login branding** says **Customize how emails and the login screen appear to your clients.**

- **Email sender name** — **Shown in the From: field on emails sent from your subdomain. Leave blank to use the platform default.** The box stops at 60 characters. Example: Your Agency Name.
- **Reply-to email** — **Where customer replies are sent. Leave blank to use the platform default.** Example: support@youragency.com. A bad address shows **Please enter a valid email address for reply-to**.
- **Login screen subtitle** — **A short line shown under your logo on the login page. Optional.** The box stops at 120 characters. Example: Welcome to our client portal.

Click **Save brand settings**. You should see **Brand settings saved**.

## What clients see

- Your logo and brand color from **Profile**
- Your display name
- Your subdomain, or your custom domain once DNS is live
- Your login subtitle, if you set one
- Your sender name and reply-to address on emails from that portal

The email signature preview still includes **Powered by AgentClara**.

## If something goes wrong

<Accordion title="I cannot open Agency Settings">
  Settings is for the agency owner. The page says **Only agency owners can access these settings.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The logo looks soft">
  Use a square PNG or JPG. The page says **Recommended: 400x400 square**. If the file is too small or not a square, you see **For best results, use a 400x400 square image.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My subdomain says it is already in use">
  Pick another name. You will see **That subdomain is already in use**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I claimed a subdomain and now I want a different one">
  Use **Change subdomain**. The confirm box says **Your old subdomain will redirect to the new one for 30 days, then stop working. You won't be able to change again for 30 days.**
</Accordion>
