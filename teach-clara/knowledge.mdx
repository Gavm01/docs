## Open Knowledge

In the sidebar, open **Knowledge**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/knowledge](https://agentclara.app/knowledge).

The page says **Manage your knowledge base articles and response templates.**

## Add a question and answer

<Steps>

<Step title="Add the item">
Add a knowledge item. If either box is empty, you see **Question and answer are required**.

If the question is too long, you see **Question must be 500 characters or less**. If the answer is too long, you see **Answer must be 10,000 characters or less**.

Example question: `What are your business hours?`
</Step>

<Step title="Add tags">
**Tags** are comma-separated. Example: `hours, schedule, availability`.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose who can use it">
**Agent Assignment** can be **All Agents**, or you can limit it.

**Access all knowledge** says **When enabled, this agent can access all knowledge articles. Turn off to manage access per item.**
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
You should see **Knowledge item added**.

Edits show **Knowledge item updated**. Deleting shows **Knowledge item deleted**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Search later with **Search questions and answers...**

**Show in widget Help tab** uses the note **Display this article in the customer-facing Help centre**. If it is not approved, you see **This article is not yet approved — it won't appear in the Help tab until approved.**

You can also **Upload Docs** or **Load Starter Pack**.

## Smart Learning Library

Open **Learned Responses** on this page. The heading is **Smart Learning Library**.

These are saved when you correct Clara in **Chat Lab** or in the **Inbox**. Each one keeps the **Original Response** and your **Improved Response**.

The more you save, the more she sounds like your team.
