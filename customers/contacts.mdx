## Open Contacts

In the sidebar, open **Contacts**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/contacts](https://agentclara.app/contacts).

The same list opens from [https://agentclara.app/customers](https://agentclara.app/customers).

## Add someone

<Steps>

<Step title="Add the first customer">
Click **Add First Customer** when the list is empty.

The form says **Enter customer information to enable personalized interactions**.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in Basic Info">
**First Name** and **Last Name**.

Under **Company & Tags**, add **Company** and **Tags**. Tags are comma-separated. The hint is `VIP, Premium, etc.`
</Step>

<Step title="Assign an agent">
**Agent Assignment**. The box is **Assign to Agent**. Under it: **Assign this customer to a specific agent**.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
You should see **Customer created**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## The list

Columns include **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Tags**, and **Last contact**.

On a person you can **Edit**, open **Call History**, **Activity Timeline**, **Notes**, **Memory**, **Manage Tags**, **Consent**, or **Trigger Automation**.

You can also **Call**, **Export**, **Tag**, **Merge**, or **Delete**.

**Add to Pipeline** sends them into a pipeline. If you have none, you see **No pipelines found. Create one in Flows first.** See [Flows](/automations/flows).

Deleting asks you to confirm. You should see **Customer deleted**.
