## Two places to look

**Schedule** in the sidebar opens the calendar at [https://agentclara.app/launch/schedule](https://agentclara.app/launch/schedule).

**Bookings** is the appointment list at [https://agentclara.app/bookings](https://agentclara.app/bookings).

The bookings heading is **Bookings**. The line under it is **Manage appointments and schedules**.

The list says **All Appointments** and **Updates automatically every 30 seconds**.

## Read the list

Filter by **Status**:

- **Upcoming**
- **Scheduled**
- **Confirmed**
- **Completed**
- **Cancelled**
- **No Show**

Filter by **Source**:

- **Inbound**
- **Outbound**
- **Manual**
- **Chat**

Filter by **Date Range**: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next 7 Days**, or **All Dates**.

Search with **Search by customer, service...**

Columns are **Customer**, **Date & Time**, **Service**, and **Created**.

If the list is empty, you see **No appointments yet**.

## If a booking is missing

**That booking isn't in the current account — switch client and try again** means you are looking at the wrong workspace. Switch account and open Bookings again.

Clara books from chat and from calls when the calendar tools are connected. Connect calendars from **Integrations**. The hub lists Calendly, Google Calendar, and Stripe on the scheduling and payments step.
