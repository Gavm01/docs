## Open business settings

Go to [https://agentclara.app/settings](https://agentclara.app/settings).

That opens **Workspace Settings** on **Business Settings**.

## What you can edit

**Business Information** says **This information is used across your Clara AI system for customer communications**.

- **Workspace Name** — **Displayed in the header and sidebar**. The box says **e.g., Downtown Salon**
- **Business Name** — the box says **e.g., Acme Corporation**
- **Business Email** — the box says **e.g., info@company.com**
- **Business Phone** — the box says **e.g., +44 20 7123 4567**
- **Timezone**
- **Business Address** — the box says **e.g., 123 Main St, London, UK**

Click **Save Changes**. You should see **Business information saved**.

## Business hours

**Business Hours** is on the same screen. The line under it is **Clara uses these hours to handle calls, schedule bookings, and send after-hours messages**.

Click **Save Hours**. You should see **Business hours saved**.

## Your own login

**My Account** is a different tab in Workspace Settings. It is **Personal Information**: your **Full Name**, your email (**Email cannot be changed from here**), **Theme** (**Light**, **Dark**, or **System**), and **Change Password**.
