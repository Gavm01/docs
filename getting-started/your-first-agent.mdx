## Open Agents

In the sidebar, open **Agents**.

You land on [https://agentclara.app/agent](https://agentclara.app/agent).

The tabs are **Basic**, **Personality**, **Routing**, **Automation**, **Security**, and **Launch**.

<Note>
If the page says **Failed to load agent configuration**, refresh. If it still fails, email support@agentclara.ai.
</Note>

## Set the basics

<Steps>

<Step title="Basic">
Open **Basic**. Save when you change something.
</Step>

<Step title="Personality">
Open **Personality**.

**Personality Presets** is where you choose a **Personality Style**. The list is:

- **General - Balanced approach for all interactions**
- **Sales - Persuasive, appointment-focused**
- **Support - Issue resolution and assistance**
- **Booking - Appointment scheduling focused**
- **Qualification - Lead assessment and filtering**
- **Collections - Payment reminders and recovery**
- **Expert - Authoritative, detailed guidance**

The page says **This determines Clara's primary approach to conversations and which workflows are prioritized**.

**Blended Mode** says **Allow Clara to adapt by combining multiple modes with custom weights**. The weights are labeled **Mode Weights (must sum to 100%)**.
</Step>

<Step title="Write her brief">
Open [https://agentclara.app/agent/brief](https://agentclara.app/agent/brief).

The page is **Clara's brief**. It says **Everything Clara knows about your business. Change anything here and she'll use it straight away.**

**What Clara should focus on** says **Every brief needs a focus — it can't be left blank, but you can change it any time.**

**What success looks like** says **Optional — leave blank if you'd rather not set one.** The example in the box is **e.g. bookings a week**.

**House rules** says **Things Clara should always do, or never do. She follows these on every channel she handles.** Click **Add house rule**.
</Step>

<Step title="Give her answers">
Open **Knowledge** and add at least one question and answer. See [Knowledge](/teach-clara/knowledge).
</Step>

<Step title="Test her">
Open **Chat Lab** at [https://agentclara.app/test/chat](https://agentclara.app/test/chat).

Ask something you put in Knowledge. If the answer is wrong, fix the knowledge item, then ask again.

For the phone, open **Voice Lab** at [https://agentclara.app/test/voice](https://agentclara.app/test/voice).
</Step>

</Steps>

## The other tabs

**Routing** is call routing, including what happens when a call goes to voicemail. See [Call handling](/voice-calls/call-handling).

**Automation** includes **Lead Calling Rules**, which control when Clara auto-calls new leads. See [Lead webhooks](/outbound/lead-webhooks).

**Follow-ups** are their own page, not a tab here. See [Follow-ups](/teach-clara/follow-ups).
