## How agency billing works

Your clients pay for their own plan. You earn a commission when they are invoiced.

You also have an agency subscription of your own. **Cancel subscription** says **Cancels your agency workspace subscription only. Your clients keep their own subscriptions and are unaffected. You retain access until the end of the current billing period.**

Money is shown in pounds (£).

## Your subscription

Go to **Agency → Settings → Billing**. The first card is **Your Subscription**.

You will see:

- **Plan** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Status** — Active, Trial, Canceled, or Suspended
- If you are on a trial, the card says **Free trial ends in** the number of days left, then **days**. On the last day it says **Free trial ends today**. After that it says **Free trial ended**.

If nothing is on file, you will see **No active subscription on file. Contact support if this is unexpected.**

## What you charge a client

You choose this when you invite them. See [Adding Clients](/agency-setup/adding-clients).

- **Client Plan** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Setup Fee (£)** — **One-time setup fee charged at onboarding. Leave at 0 for no fee.**
- **Trial Period (days)** — the helper says **Days before monthly billing starts. 0 = billing starts immediately after setup.** The box allows 0 to 365.
- **Require card at trial start** — **If enabled, client enters payment details at signup. Trial converts to paid automatically at trial end. If disabled, client uses trial free and must actively upgrade to continue.**

The plan on the form starts from **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**. On that tab you can also set:

- **Default max agents** — the placeholder is **Leave empty for no cap**. Under it: **Empty = no per-client cap. Integer = enforced per-client cap (overrides the plan limit).**
- **Default max calls per month** — **Empty = no per-client cap. Integer = cap applied at invite time.**

A single invite can override those defaults.

## Commission

You start earning when a client is invoiced. Until then, commission history says **No commissions yet. You will start earning when your clients are invoiced.**

### On Clients

**Agency → Clients** shows four numbers:

- **Total Clients**, with your cap beside the number. It reads `/` then your maximum, then `max`.
- **Total Earned**
- **Pending Earnings**
- **Stripe Payouts** — **Active**, or a button to finish Stripe

### On Home

**Agency → Home** shows **MRR** (monthly recurring revenue), **Pending payout**, and **Commission this month**.

### On a client

Open the client from **Action items** on **Home**. The **Billing** card shows **Commission this month**, **Lifetime commission**, and **Last paid**.

### On Revenue

Go to **Agency → Revenue**, or [https://agentclara.app/agency/revenue](https://agentclara.app/agency/revenue).

The page is **Revenue**. The line under it is **Your commissions, payouts, and revenue history**.

- **Commission this month**
- **Last month**
- **Year to date**
- **Last paid out** — or **Not yet paid out**
- **Revenue trend**
- **Top clients** — switch between **This month** and **Lifetime**
- **Payout history**
- **Commission history** — date, client, gross, commission, and status. Filter it, then **Export CSV**.

### On Settings → Billing

Under **Commission Summary**:

- **Earned this month**
- **Total paid to date**
- **Last payout** — or **No payouts yet**
- **Estimated next payout** — or **No pending earnings**

**Commission History** is a table: **Client**, **Invoice amount**, **Rate**, **Commission**, **Status**, and **Date**.

Statuses are **Pending**, **Batched**, **Paid**, **Refunded**, and **Failed**. If that client's status is churned, the name shows **(churned)**.

Filter with **Filter by status**.

## Get paid

Commission is paid to a Stripe account that you connect.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open payouts">
Go to **Agency → Settings → Billing**. Find **Payout Account (Stripe)**.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect Stripe">
Click **Connect Stripe**.

The status moves through **Pending verification**. The line under that is **Stripe is reviewing your account. This usually takes a few minutes.** Then **Connected** and **Ready to receive payouts.**
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the payout">
Payouts are monthly. If nothing has been paid yet, **Payout history** on **Revenue** says **No payouts yet. Your first payout will land on the 1st of next month after you've earned at least £20.**

If Stripe is not connected, that section says **Connect your Stripe account to start receiving monthly payouts.**
</Step>

</Steps>

If the connection fails, click **Retry**. If it later says **Account disconnected**, the line is **Your Stripe account is no longer linked. Reconnect to resume payouts.** Click **Reconnect**.

## Cancel your agency subscription

This is on **Agency → Settings → Danger Zone**.

**Cancel subscription** says **Cancels your agency workspace subscription only. Your clients keep their own subscriptions and are unaffected. You retain access until the end of the current billing period.**

If there is nothing to cancel, you will see **No active subscription**. If it is already cancelled, the page says **Your subscription is cancelled. You'll keep full access until** the date.

## If something goes wrong

<Accordion title="I invited a client and commission is still empty">
  Commission history says **No commissions yet. You will start earning when your clients are invoiced.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stripe says Pending verification">
  The card says **Stripe is reviewing your account. This usually takes a few minutes.** If it fails, click **Retry**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I have earnings but no payout">
  If nothing has been paid yet, the page says **No payouts yet. Your first payout will land on the 1st of next month after you've earned at least £20.** Stripe also has to be **Connected**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A client payment failed">
  **Home → Action items** shows **Payment failing** or **In grace period**, with **Send payment reminder** or **Review grace period** as the suggested step. Open the client from that list.
</Accordion>
