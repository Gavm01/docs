## Open business settings

Go to [https://agentclara.app/settings](https://agentclara.app/settings).

That opens **Workspace Settings** on **Business Settings**.

## What you can edit

**Business Information** is used across Clara for customer communications.

- **Workspace Name** — shown in the header and sidebar. Example: Downtown Salon
- **Business Name** — example: Acme Corporation
- **Business Email** — example: info@company.com
- **Business Phone** — example: +44 20 7123 4567
- **Timezone**
- **Business Address**

Save. You should see **Business information saved**.

## Business hours

**Business Hours** is on the same screen. Clara uses these hours to handle calls, schedule bookings, and send after-hours messages.

Save the hours. You should see **Business hours saved**.

## Your own login

**My Account** is a different tab in Workspace Settings. It is **Personal Information**: your **Full Name**, your email (you cannot change the email here), **Theme** (**Light**, **Dark**, or **System**), and **Change Password**.
