## SMS uses your phone number

Texts go through a number on **Phone Numbers**, not a separate SMS app.

Open [https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers](https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers).

## Turn SMS on

<Steps>

<Step title="Get a mobile number">
Click **Get a Number**.

The page says **SMS is only available on mobile numbers — local and toll-free numbers are voice-only.**

See [Setting up voice](/voice-calls/setting-up-voice) for the search and purchase steps. Additional numbers are **£1.50/month**.
</Step>

<Step title="Enable SMS">
On the number, turn on **Enable SMS for outbound messages**.

If SMS is off, the page says outbound messages will use the system default number.
</Step>

<Step title="Read replies in the Inbox">
Open **Inbox**. Use the **SMS** channel to see those conversations.
</Step>

</Steps>

## More than one number

Each number has its own **Voice**, **SMS**, and **WhatsApp** settings.

**Set Outbound** chooses the number used for outbound messages. **Make Default** sets the default.
