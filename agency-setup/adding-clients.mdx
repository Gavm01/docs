## Invite a client

Each client gets their own Clara account. You invite them by email. They open the link and finish setup themselves.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Clients">
In the sidebar, open **Agency**, then **Clients**.

Or go to [https://agentclara.app/agency/clients](https://agentclara.app/agency/clients).

The big title is your agency business name. If that name is not set yet, the title is **Reseller Dashboard**. The line under the title is **Reseller Dashboard**. The button in the top right is **+ Invite Client**.
</Step>

<Step title="Click + Invite Client">
The form is titled **Invite a Client**.

The line under the title is **Send an invite to a new client. They'll receive an email link to set up their account.**
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the client">
**Client Name** is their business name. The box says **Acme Corp**.

**Client Email** is the person who will own that account. The box says **client@example.com**.

**Client Country** starts on **United Kingdom**. Change it if they are based somewhere else. The list includes the United States, Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, India, and South Africa.

**Client Plan** is **Starter**, **Growth**, **Scale**, or **Enterprise**. It starts on the default you saved under **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the setup fee and trial">
**Setup Fee (£)** says **One-time setup fee charged at onboarding. Leave at 0 for no fee.**

**Trial Period (days)** — the helper says **Days before monthly billing starts. 0 = billing starts immediately after setup.** The box allows 0 to 365.

**Require card at trial start**. The helper says **If enabled, client enters payment details at signup. Trial converts to paid automatically at trial end. If disabled, client uses trial free and must actively upgrade to continue.**
</Step>

<Step title="Send the invite">
Click **Send Invite**.

You should see **Invite sent**, then **Invitation sent to** their email. The list then shows that client as **Pending**. Under the name it says **Awaiting** and their email address.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
This invite is for a client business. To add someone who works at your agency, use **Agency → Team** and **Invite member**.
</Note>

## While they are pending

A pending client is faded, with a yellow dot and a **Pending** badge.

Click **Resend Invite** if they did not get the email. You should see **Invite resent!** Under it: **A new invite email has been sent to your client.** If the email still does not go out, you see **Email not sent** and **The invite is still there, but the email did not go out. Try Resend again.**

When they accept, the row is no longer faded. A green dot means the status is active. A blue dot means the status is trial. Under the name, the app shows the plan tier, a dot, and the status. On the right it shows the tier plan and **Since** the date they joined.

## Defaults for the next invite

Go to **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**.

The line under the title is **Default values applied to new client invites. Per-invite values override these.**

- **Default plan tier** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Default max agents** — the placeholder is **Leave empty for no cap**. Under it: **Empty = no per-client cap. Integer = enforced per-client cap (overrides the plan limit).**
- **Default max calls per month** — **Empty = no per-client cap. Integer = cap applied at invite time.**

Click **Save**. You should see **Defaults saved**. If it fails, you see **Could not save defaults. Please try again.**

## Check a client later

**Clients** is the full list, including people who have not accepted yet.

**Home** ([https://agentclara.app/agency/home](https://agentclara.app/agency/home)) shows who needs you. Under **Action items**, click the client name. That opens their page.

On that page you can see:

- **Client health** — **Healthy**, **Watch**, **At risk**, or **Critical**. The line under it is **Payment 40% + engagement 30% + usage 30%**. If there is no score, you see **Health score not available**.
- **Billing** — **Commission this month**, **Lifetime commission**, and **Last paid**
- **Recent activity** — if the list is empty, you see **No activity in the last 30 days.**

Health also shows as a badge on the **Clients** list. Hover it to see the reason: **Payment failed**, **In billing grace period**, **Trial ends in &lt;3 days**, **No usage in 7+ days**, **First message not sent**, or **No escalation contact set**. If there is no reason, you see **No active signals**.

## If something goes wrong

<Accordion title="The invite saved, but they got no email">
  You will see **Invite saved**. Under it: **The invite was saved, but the email did not go out. Use Resend to try again.** Find the pending row and click **Resend Invite**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Send Invite says the email is invalid">
  Use a normal email address, like `name@company.com`. You will see **Please enter a valid email address**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cannot find a client I already invited">
  Open **Agency → Clients**. Pending invites stay on that list until they accept. Use **Load More Clients** if the list is long.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The client page says Client not found">
  That client may have been removed. Go back with **Back to clients**.
</Accordion>
