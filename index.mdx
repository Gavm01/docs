## Meet Clara

Clara is the assistant inside AgentClara. She answers customers, books appointments, and follows up. You see the work in one inbox.

She can talk by text, email, Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, your website chat, and the phone.

## Get started

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your account">
Go to [https://agentclara.app](https://agentclara.app) and sign up.
</Step>

<Step title="Teach Clara">
Open **Agents** and set how she should sound. Then open **Knowledge** and add the answers she should use.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect a way in">
Open **Integrations** for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or your CRM. Or open **Phone Numbers** and get a number. Or add the **Chat Widget** to your website.
</Step>

<Step title="Try her yourself">
Open **Chat Lab** and send a message before a customer does. For the phone, open **Voice Lab**.
</Step>

</Steps>
