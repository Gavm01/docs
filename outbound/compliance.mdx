## Open the compliance screen

Go to [https://agentclara.app/compliance](https://agentclara.app/compliance).

You can also open **Settings**, then **Compliance & Privacy**, from Workspace Settings. Opening [https://agentclara.app/settings/compliance](https://agentclara.app/settings/compliance) takes you there.

The heading is **Compliance & Security**.

If you use more than one workspace, select the account first. Otherwise you see **Select a sub-account before saving compliance settings**.

## What you can set

**Compliance Frameworks** include switches for **HIPAA Compliance** and the other modes listed on the page, such as GDPR. Turning a mode on records that you want those safeguards. It does not file anything with a regulator for you.

**Data Retention** asks for a **Data Retention Period (days)** and an **Audit Log Retention (days)**. The screen says these periods are **not enforced**. Audit logs are not removed on a schedule, so they are kept.

**PII Protection & Redaction** has **Auto-Redact PII**. The screen says this records your preference and **redaction does not currently run on its own**. Rules can be **Mask**, **Hash**, or **Remove**.

**Security Controls** includes **Encrypt Call Recordings**. That encrypts voice recordings at rest.

Click save. You should see **Settings saved**.

## What this page does not do

This screen does not check calling lists, quiet hours, or local phone laws for you. If you run [Broadcasts](/outbound/overview), you still have to follow the calling rules that apply to your business.

**Compliance Reports** is marked **Coming Soon**.
