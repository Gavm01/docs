## Open Phone Numbers

Go to [https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers](https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers).

The page title is **Phone Numbers**. The line under it is **Manage your phone numbers and SMS settings**.

Until you add one, you see **No phone number added yet** and **Add a number to start sending SMS and making calls from your own line.**

## Get a number

<Steps>

<Step title="Click Get a Number">
That opens [https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers/get](https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers/get).

The page says **Search and purchase phone numbers for your agents**. Additional numbers are **£1.50/month**.
</Step>

<Step title="Search">
Pick a **Country**. The list is the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Ireland, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Poland, Portugal, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, New Zealand, Mexico, Brazil, Israel, and the United Arab Emirates.

Pick a **Number Type**:

- **Local (Landline)** — **Local numbers are landlines — they work for voice calls but can't send or receive texts.**
- **Mobile** — **Mobile numbers support both voice and SMS messaging.**
- **Toll-Free** — **Toll-free numbers are free for callers, voice-only in most regions.**

The page also says **Tip: Adding an area code (like 212 or 415) usually shows more options.**

**SMS is only available on mobile numbers — local and toll-free numbers are voice-only.**

Click **Search Numbers**.
</Step>

<Step title="Buy it">
Click **Purchase**. If the number needs a check first, the button is **Verify & Purchase**.

Some numbers need an address. The form asks for **Business / Customer Name**, **Street Address**, **City**, **Region / State**, **Postal Code**, and **Country**.

When it works, you see **Number purchased successfully!** and **Your verified number is now ready to use.**
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
If you see **Phone Number Limit Reached**, the line under it is **Upgrade your plan to add more phone numbers.** If you see **You don't have permission to purchase phone numbers**, ask an account owner.
</Note>

## If you already have a Twilio number

On **Phone Numbers**, click **From Twilio**.

The page says **Import phone numbers you already own in your Twilio account.**

**From SIP Trunk** is also on that page. Choosing it says **SIP trunk configuration requires additional setup**.

## After the number is on the list

Each row can show **Voice**, **SMS**, and **WhatsApp** as yes or no.

- **Enable SMS for outbound messages** if you want texts from that number. If SMS is off, the page says **SMS is off — outbound messages will use the system default number.**
- **Set Outbound** or **Remove Outbound** chooses whether this number places outbound calls and SMS.
- **Make Default** sets the default number.

The page says **When a customer calls any of your Clara numbers, she picks up automatically according to your call forwarding rules (set in Agent settings).**

It also says **Your phone type (mobile, system, landline) affects how call forwarding works. This is set in Agent setup and helps your phone provider route calls correctly.**
