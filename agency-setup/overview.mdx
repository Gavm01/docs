## Run an agency on AgentClara

An agency account is for businesses that serve clients, not only their own customers.

You invite each client. They get their own Clara account and their own subscription. Commission history says **No commissions yet. You will start earning when your clients are invoiced.** You can put your logo, color, and web address on the portal they log in to.

## Start as an agency

Go to [https://agentclara.app/start/agency](https://agentclara.app/start/agency).

Tell Clara you serve clients. When your own setup is live, the next job is **Add your first client**. You will also see **Set up your white-label** and **Invite a teammate**.

## Home

**Home** says **Welcome back** and **Your reseller dashboard**.

The numbers at the top are **Active clients**, **MRR**, **Pending payout**, and **Commission this month**.

**Recent activity**. If it is empty, you will see **No recent activity yet.**

**Action items** lists clients who need attention. Click a name to open that client. If everyone is fine, you will see **No clients need attention right now.**

Each item can show a suggested next step, such as **Send payment reminder**, **Review grace period**, **Convert trial**, **Check in with client**, **Complete onboarding**, or **Set escalation contact**.

**Quick links** go to **View all clients**, **Revenue & commissions**, and **Settings**.

## Your agency team

Go to **Agency → Team**.

Click **Invite member**. The line under it is **Send an email invitation to join your agency team.** Enter their email and a role:

- **Owner**
- **Admin**
- **Member**

The hint under the roles is **Admins can invite and remove members. Members can view all clients.**

Click **Send invitation**. Until they accept, they sit under **Pending invitations**, where you can **Resend** or **Revoke**.

To remove someone later, use **Remove member**. The confirm title is **Remove team member?** The line under it is **Are you sure you want to remove** their name **from your team? They will lose access to all agency data.**

## Words the product uses

**Client** — a business you invited. They have their own login, their own plan, and their own subscription.

**Plan** — **Starter**, **Growth**, **Scale**, or **Enterprise**. You pick this on the invite. You can set a default under **Client Defaults**.

**Commission** — your share of a client invoice. You see it on **Home**, **Clients**, **Revenue**, and the client page.

**Agency owner** — the account that can open **Settings**. If you are not the owner, Settings says **You don't have access to agency settings.**
