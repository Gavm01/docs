## Invite a client

Each client gets their own Clara account. You invite them by email. They open the link and finish setup themselves.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Clients">
In the sidebar, open **Agency**, then **Clients**.

Or go to [https://agentclara.app/agency/clients](https://agentclara.app/agency/clients).

The page title is your agency name. The button in the top right is **+ Invite Client**.
</Step>

<Step title="Click + Invite Client">
The form is titled **Invite a Client**.

They receive an email link to set up their account.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the client">
**Client Name** is their business name. Example: Acme Corp.

**Client Email** is the person who will own that account. Example: client@example.com.

**Client Country** starts on **United Kingdom**. Change it if they are based somewhere else. The list includes the United States, Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, India, and South Africa.

**Client Plan** is **Starter**, **Growth**, **Scale**, or **Enterprise**. It starts on the default you saved under **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the setup fee and trial">
**Setup Fee (£)** is a one-time fee charged at onboarding. Leave it at `0` for no fee.

**Trial Period (days)** is how many days they get before monthly billing starts. `0` means billing starts as soon as setup is done. The maximum is 365 days.

**Require card at trial start** is optional.

- Turn it on and they enter payment details at signup. The trial becomes a paid plan on its own when the trial ends.
- Leave it off and the trial is free. They have to choose a paid plan themselves to keep going.
</Step>

<Step title="Send the invite">
Click **Send Invite**.

You should see **Invite sent**. The list then shows that client as **Pending**, with **Awaiting** and their email address.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
This invite is for a client business. To add someone who works at your agency, use **Agency → Team** and **Invite member**.
</Note>

## While they are pending

A pending client is faded, with a yellow dot and a **Pending** badge.

Click **Resend Invite** if they did not get the email. You should see **Invite resent!**

When they accept, the row turns solid. A green dot means **active**. A blue dot means **trial**. Under the name you see their plan and status, for example `growth · active`, plus the plan name and the date they joined.

## Defaults for the next invite

Go to **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**.

These values are applied to new invites. Anything you type on a single invite overrides them.

- **Default plan tier** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Default max agents** — leave empty for no cap. A number is a per-client cap and overrides the plan limit.
- **Default max calls per month** — leave empty for no cap. A number is applied when you send the invite.

Click **Save**. You should see **Defaults saved**.

## Check a client later

**Clients** is the full list, including people who have not accepted yet.

**Home** ([https://agentclara.app/agency/home](https://agentclara.app/agency/home)) shows who needs you. Under **Action items**, click the client name. That opens their page.

On that page you can see:

- **Client health** — Healthy, At risk, or Critical. The score is payment 40%, engagement 30%, and usage 30%.
- **Billing** — commission this month, lifetime commission, and last paid
- **Recent activity** — what happened in the last 30 days

Health also shows as a badge on the **Clients** list. Hover it to see the reason, such as **Payment failed**, **In billing grace period**, **Trial ends in under 3 days**, **No usage in 7+ days**, **First message not sent**, or **No escalation contact set**.

## If something goes wrong

<Accordion title="The invite saved, but they got no email">
  You will see **Invite saved**. The invite is still there. Find the pending row and click **Resend Invite**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Send Invite says the email is invalid">
  Use a normal email address, like `name@company.com`. The form rejects addresses without an `@` and a domain.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cannot find a client I already invited">
  Open **Agency → Clients**. Pending invites stay on that list until they accept. Use **Load More Clients** if the list is long.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The client page says Client not found">
  That client may have been removed. Go back with **Back to clients**.
</Accordion>
