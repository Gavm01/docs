## Meet Clara

Clara answers customers for you. She uses what you teach her, then you see the conversation in the **Inbox**.

You stay in charge. You can read every conversation, take over, and correct her.

## What she can do

**Answer from your knowledge.** Add questions and answers under **Knowledge**. She uses them in chat and on calls.

**Book appointments.** Customers can book by chat or by phone. You review them under **Bookings** and on **Schedule**.

**Use the channels you connect.** SMS, email, Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, the website **Chat Widget**, and voice. They show up in the **Inbox**.

**Follow up.** **Follow-ups** can message someone after a call. **Broadcasts** is where outbound campaigns live.

**Learn from corrections.** When you fix an answer, you can save it in the **Smart Learning Library** on the Knowledge page.
