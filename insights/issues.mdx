## Open Issues

In the sidebar, open **Issues**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/issues](https://agentclara.app/issues).

The heading is **Issues**. The line under it is **Track and manage system issues with AI-powered insights**.

The views are **Issue Tracking**, **Tickets**, **Escalations**, and **Clara Failsafe**.

**Clara Failsafe** detects when Clara is offline or not responding, and alerts you.

## The counts

At the top you see **Total Issues**, **Open**, **Escalated**, **Resolved**, and **Avg. Resolution Time**.

## Find or create one

Search with **Search issues by title, description, or category...**

Filter **Status**: **Investigating**, **Open**, **Escalated**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**.

Filter **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Critical**.

Click **Create Issue** to add one. Click **Export CSV** to download the list.

If the list is empty, you see **No issues found**.

## On an issue

You can read **Likely Cause**, **Error Pattern**, **Affected Flows**, and **Linked Conversations**.

**Suggested Fix** and **Apply Fix** are the resolution tools on the issue. **View Relevant Flow Step** jumps to the flow.
