## Open billing

Go to [https://agentclara.app/billing](https://agentclara.app/billing).

The heading is **Billing Overview**.

You can also open **Settings**, then **Billing & Usage**.

This page is for your own subscription. Agency commission and client plans are in [Agency billing](/agency-setup/billing).

## What you see

- **Current Plan**
- **Trial**, if you are in one
- **Next Billing Date**
- **Last Invoice**

If you have free credit, the card title is **Your £100 free credit**. While it loads, you see **Checking how much is left…**. Then you see either **Your £100 free credit is active.** or the pounds still left **of your £100 is left. You've used** how many are used **of** the limit. The next line is **Your free credit ends on** the date, then **You won't be charged the subscription fee until then.**

**Payment Failed – Action Required**. Under it you see **We couldn't process your payment. You have until** the date, then **(** how many days are left, written as day or days, **remaining) to update your card before your access is paused.** Click **Update Payment Method**.

## What gets billed

**What Counts as a Billable Resolution** includes:

- **✓ Inbound call where Clara books/changes/cancels an appointment**
- **✓ Inbound call where Clara answers the question and call ends successfully**
- **✓ Chat conversation where Clara resolves the issue**
- **✓ Outbound call where Clara reaches the customer and completes a follow-up or upsell**
- **✓ Meaningful interaction where AI handles end-to-end (no human transfer)**

**Non-Billable Cases (£0.00)** are:

- **✗ Transfer to human agent (no AI resolution)**
- **✗ Call timeout / customer hang-up before resolution**
- **✗ Spam / robocall / test interactions**
- **✗ Technical errors / failed calls**
- **✗ Duplicate interactions (e.g. customer calls back about same issue within 1 hour)**

The page also says **• Maximum 1 charge per resolved case**.

## Invoices

**Invoices & Payments** says **Billing is automatic via Stripe. Invoices are generated and emailed monthly.**

The history columns include **Date**, **Period Start**, **Period End**, **Plan Fee (£)**, **Interactions**, **Overage (£)**, **Wallet Top-ups (£)**, **Total (£)**, and **Status**.

Click **Download Invoices** for a file. Click **Add Payment Method** to add a card. That opens a secure payment page.

If you cancelled, **Resubscribe** is on this page.

**Resolution Log** says **All AI resolutions with billing explanations — why each was charged or free**.
