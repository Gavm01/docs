## Open the compliance screen

Go to [https://agentclara.app/compliance](https://agentclara.app/compliance).

You can also open **Settings**, then **Compliance & Privacy**, from Workspace Settings. Opening [https://agentclara.app/settings/compliance](https://agentclara.app/settings/compliance) takes you there.

The heading is **Compliance & Security**. The line under it is **Enterprise-grade security, compliance, and governance controls**.

Across the top, the cards are labeled **HIPAA**, **SOC 2**, and **GDPR**. Each one shows **Active** or **Inactive**. **Audit Logs** shows a number of days.

**Security Score** says **Visual indicator based on encryption, retention, PII protection, and authentication settings.** The words under the score are **Needs improvement**, **Good security posture**, or **Excellent security posture**.

The tabs are **Compliance Modes**, **Data Management**, **PII Protection**, and **Security**.

If you use more than one workspace, select the account first. Saving without one shows **Select a sub-account before saving compliance settings**. Changing a switch without one shows **Select a sub-account before changing compliance settings**.

## What you can set

On **Compliance Modes**, the screen says **Root Workspace Settings:** and **The settings below apply to your root workspace. Use the sub-account controls above for per-account settings.**

**Compliance Frameworks** has a switch labeled **HIPAA Compliance**. The line under it is **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act**.

The box under that starts with **What this means:** and then **Activates HIPAA safeguards including encryption, audit trails, and access controls for handling protected health information (PHI).** That box is text on the screen. AgentClara itself is not HIPAA or SOC 2 compliant. The database the app runs on, Supabase, is the compliant part. Turning a switch on, or seeing **Active**, does not certify your Clara account. It does not file anything with a regulator for you.

**Data Management:** says **Record your intended retention and audit log periods.**

**Data Retention Period (days) — not enforced** says **Records how long you intend to keep conversation data. Nothing is deleted when this period elapses — conversation data is removed only when you ask us to erase it.**

**Audit Log Retention (days) — not enforced** says **Records how long you intend to keep audit logs (365 days is a common choice). Audit logs are not currently removed on a schedule, so they are kept indefinitely.**

**PII Protection:** says **Automatically redact sensitive or regulated data from transcripts.**

**PII Protection & Redaction** has **Auto-Redact PII — not enforced**. The screen says: **Records your preference for redacting personally identifiable information. Redaction does not currently run on its own.** Rules can be **Mask**, **Hash**, or **Remove**.

**Security:** says **Manage call recording encryption settings.**

**Security Controls** includes **Encrypt Call Recordings**. The line under it is **Encrypt all voice call recordings at rest**. The box under that starts with **What this means:** and then **Protects sensitive voice recordings with industry-standard encryption for privacy and compliance.** That box is text on the screen. It is not a certificate.

Click **Save All Settings**. You should see **Settings saved**. If it fails, you see **Failed to save compliance settings**. If a switch fails to save on its own, you see **Failed to auto-save compliance setting**.

## Security suggestions

**Security Suggestions** says **General hardening steps worth considering. This is a fixed list — it does not reflect what is currently switched on for your account.**

The list is **Enable 2FA for all team members**, **Turn on transcript encryption**, and **Enable audit log export**.

## What this page does not do

This screen does not check calling lists, quiet hours, or local phone laws for you. If you run [Broadcasts](/outbound/overview), you still have to follow the calling rules that apply to your business.

**Compliance Reports** is marked **Coming Soon**.
