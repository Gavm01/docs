## Clara on the phone

Clara can answer calls to your number and place outbound calls. You test her in **Voice Lab** before customers hear her.

Calls use a number from **Phone Numbers**. Inbound calls follow the call forwarding rules in **Agent** settings.

## Do this first

<Steps>

<Step title="Get a number">
Open [Phone Numbers](https://agentclara.app/integrations/phone-numbers) and click **Get a Number**.

See [Setting up voice](/voice-calls/setting-up-voice).
</Step>

<Step title="Set call behaviour">
Open **Agents**, then the **Routing** tab.

That is **Call Routing**. It includes **Voicemail Handling**. See [Call handling](/voice-calls/call-handling).
</Step>

<Step title="Hear her yourself">
Open **Voice Lab** at [https://agentclara.app/test/voice](https://agentclara.app/test/voice).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Where calls show up

**Voice** is one of the channels in the **Inbox**, next to SMS, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and the chat widget.

Outbound calling campaigns live under **Broadcasts**, not on this page. See [Broadcasts](/outbound/overview).
