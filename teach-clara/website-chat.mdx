## Open Chat Widget

Go to [https://agentclara.app/integrations/chat-widget](https://agentclara.app/integrations/chat-widget).

The heading is **Chat Widget**. The line under it is **Customize and embed your chat widget on any website**.

## Create the widget

<Steps>

<Step title="Click Create Widget">
If you have more than one, pick the one you are editing.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect an agent">
**Agent Connection** asks **Select which agent powers this chat widget**.

The agent's personality, knowledge, and AI settings are what visitors get.

If you see **No agent connected. The widget won't be able to respond to messages.** The badge is **Live** or **Disabled**. Hover **Disabled** and it says **Enable this agent in the Agents tab**.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the look">
Open **Appearance**.

- **Widget Name**
- **Agent Display Name**
- **Primary Color** and **Button Color**
- **Position**: **Bottom Right**, **Bottom Left**, **Top Right**, or **Top Left**
- **Border Radius**
</Step>

<Step title="Set the messages">
The **Messages** section says **Widget messages take priority over agent settings**.

**Welcome Message** says **This message is shown when a visitor opens the chat. It overrides the agent's first message.** The starter text is `Hi! How can I help you today?`

**Away Message** says **Shown when outside operating hours (if enabled).** The starter text is `We're currently away. Leave a message and we'll get back to you!`

**Input Placeholder** starts as `Type a message...`
</Step>

<Step title="Choose who sees it">
**Audience** has three groups:

- **Visitors** — **Anyone who visits your site but is not logged in and has no conversation history**
- **Leads** — **Anyone who starts a conversation with you or replies to an outbound message**
- **Users** — **Anyone who has signed up to your product or logged in to an existing account**
</Step>

<Step title="Choose when it opens">
**Triggers** includes:

- **Enabled** — **Widget is visible on your website**
- **Exit Intent** — **Open widget when user tries to leave**
- **Mobile Enabled** — **Show widget on mobile devices**
- **Auto-open Delay (seconds)** — the box says **Leave empty to disable**
- **Scroll Trigger (%)** — the box says **Leave empty to disable**
</Step>

<Step title="Embed it">
Open **Embed** and copy the code. You should see **Embed code copied!**

Paste that code on your website.
</Step>

</Steps>

Use **Preview** on the page before you publish the site.

Chats from the widget show in the **Inbox** under **Chat Widget**.
