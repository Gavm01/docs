## How agency billing works

Your clients pay for their own plan. You earn a commission when they are invoiced.

You also have an agency subscription of your own. Cancelling that subscription does not cancel your clients. They keep their plans.

Money is shown in pounds (£).

## Your subscription

Go to **Agency → Settings → Billing**. The first card is **Your Subscription**.

You will see:

- **Plan** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Status** — Active, Trial, Canceled, or Suspended
- If you are on a trial, how many days are left. The card says **Free trial ends today** or **Free trial ended** when that is the case.

If nothing is on file, you will see **No active subscription on file. Contact support if this is unexpected.**

## What you charge a client

You choose this when you invite them. See [Adding Clients](/agency-setup/adding-clients).

- **Client Plan** — Starter, Growth, Scale, or Enterprise
- **Setup Fee (£)** — a one-time fee at onboarding, or `0` for none
- **Trial Period (days)** — `0` to 365. `0` means billing starts as soon as setup is done.
- **Require card at trial start** — they either enter a card now, or they stay on a free trial until they choose to pay

The plan on the form starts from **Agency → Settings → Client Defaults**. On that tab you can also set:

- **Default max agents** — empty means no cap. A number overrides the plan limit for that client.
- **Default max calls per month** — empty means no cap. A number is applied when you send the invite.

A single invite can override those defaults.

## Commission

You start earning when a client is invoiced. Until then, commission history says **No commissions yet. You will start earning when your clients are invoiced.**

### On Clients

**Agency → Clients** shows four numbers:

- **Total Clients**, with your cap beside the number (it reads like `/ 50 max`)
- **Total Earned**
- **Pending Earnings**
- **Stripe Payouts** — **Active**, or a button to finish Stripe

### On Home

**Agency → Home** shows **MRR** (monthly recurring revenue), **Pending payout**, and **Commission this month**.

### On a client

Open the client from **Action items** on **Home**. The **Billing** card shows **Commission this month**, **Lifetime commission**, and **Last paid**.

### On Revenue

Go to **Agency → Revenue**, or [https://agentclara.app/agency/revenue](https://agentclara.app/agency/revenue).

The page is **Revenue**. The line under it is **Your commissions, payouts, and revenue history**.

- **Commission this month**
- **Last month**
- **Year to date**
- **Last paid out** — or **Not yet paid out**
- **Revenue trend**
- **Top clients** — switch between **This month** and **Lifetime**
- **Payout history**
- **Commission history** — date, client, gross, commission, and status. Filter it, then **Export CSV**.

### On Settings → Billing

Under **Commission Summary**:

- **Earned this month**
- **Total paid to date**
- **Last payout** — or **No payouts yet**
- **Estimated next payout** — or **No pending earnings**

**Commission History** is a table: **Client**, **Invoice amount**, **Rate**, **Commission**, **Status**, and **Date**.

Statuses are **Pending**, **Batched**, **Paid**, **Refunded**, and **Failed**. A client who has left can be tagged **(churned)**.

Filter with **Filter by status**.

## Get paid

Commission is paid to a Stripe account that you connect.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open payouts">
Go to **Agency → Settings → Billing**. Find **Payout Account (Stripe)**.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect Stripe">
Click **Connect Stripe**.

The status moves through **Pending verification** (Stripe usually takes a few minutes), then **Connected** and **Ready to receive payouts.**
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the payout">
Payouts are monthly. **Payout history** on **Revenue** says your first payout lands on the 1st of the next month after you have earned at least £20.

If Stripe is not connected, that section says **Connect your Stripe account to start receiving monthly payouts.**
</Step>

</Steps>

If the connection fails, click **Retry**. If it later says **Account disconnected**, click **Reconnect**.

## Cancel your agency subscription

This is on **Agency → Settings → Danger Zone**.

**Cancel subscription** cancels your agency workspace subscription only. Your clients keep their own subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

If there is nothing to cancel, you will see **No active subscription**. If it is already cancelled, the page tells you the date you keep access until.

## If something goes wrong

<Accordion title="I invited a client and commission is still empty">
  Commission appears when that client is invoiced. A trial with no invoice yet does not create a row.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stripe says Pending verification">
  Wait a few minutes. The card says Stripe is reviewing your account. If it fails, click **Retry**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I have earnings but no payout">
  The first payout waits until you have earned at least £20, then pays on the 1st of the next month. Stripe also has to be **Connected**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A client payment failed">
  **Home → Action items** shows **Payment failing** or **In grace period**, with **Send payment reminder** or **Review grace period** as the suggested step. Open the client from that list.
</Accordion>
