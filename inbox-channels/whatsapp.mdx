## Where to start

Open **Integrations**. The page is the **Integrations Hub**.

You can also open [Socials & Apps](https://agentclara.app/integrations/socials-apps).

WhatsApp sits with Facebook and Instagram under communications. You need permission to change integrations. If you do not, you see **You don't have permission to change integrations.**

## Connect WhatsApp

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the WhatsApp card">
Start from the WhatsApp card. The helper is titled **Set up WhatsApp Business**.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the display name">
**WhatsApp display name** is your business name. This is what customers see.

The helper says **Meta reviews it after setup. If it's rejected, connecting has to be done again with a different name — so it's worth getting right now.**
</Step>

<Step title="Finish in Meta">
The screen says **Meta will ask for a phone number on the next screen. Copy yours below — then Meta will text it a verification code.**

Click **Copy code** if you need to paste the code. You should see **Code copied**.

When Meta has the number, the heading is **Enter your WhatsApp PIN**. The line under it is **Meta has the number. Type the 6-digit PIN you set for it. That finishes the connection.**
</Step>

<Step title="Wait until it says connected">
You should see **WhatsApp connected** or **WhatsApp connected successfully**.

If it still says **Still registering**, the line is **Still registering — safe to leave this page.** Then **You can close this — we'll keep watching, and we'll email you when it's connected or if something needs your attention.**
</Step>

</Steps>

## If it fails

**Meta couldn't connect this number**. Then **Here's what came back:** and **Nothing was charged and nothing is half-connected. You can start again from the WhatsApp card, or reply to this and we'll take a look.**

**Could not load Facebook** means refresh the page. If a popup was blocked, allow popups and try again.

Messages then show in the **Inbox** under **WhatsApp**.
