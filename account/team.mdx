## Open team management

Go to [https://agentclara.app/users](https://agentclara.app/users).

The heading is **Team Management**. The line under it is **Manage users, roles, and access levels for your workspace**.

You can also open **Settings**, then **Users & Permissions**. That item is for admins. If you are not an admin, the users page says **You need admin privileges to access this page.**

This is your own team. Agency staff are invited from **Agency**, then **Team**. See [Adding clients](/agency-setup/adding-clients).

## Invite someone

<Steps>

<Step title="Click Invite User">
If you are at your plan limit, you see **You've reached your plan's user limit. Upgrade your plan to add more users.**
</Step>

<Step title="Enter their details">
**Full Name** and **Email Address**.

**Permission Level** is **User** or **Admin**.

You cannot change your own permission level.
</Step>

<Step title="Send it">
They show under **Pending Invitations** as **Invited** until they join.

You can **Resend** or **Cancel** an invite. A used-up invite shows **Expired**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## After they join

**Team Members** shows **Active**, **Invited**, or **Suspended**.

On a person you can **Edit User**, open **Permissions** and **Feature Access**, **Disable User**, **Reset Password**, or **Delete User**.

**Save Changes** confirms an edit. You should see **User information updated**.
