## Two different template screens

Clara has two template tools. They are not the same page.

**Smart Outbound Templates** live under **Broadcasts**, on the **Templates** tab.

**Response Templates** live at [https://agentclara.app/templates](https://agentclara.app/templates). Those are reusable replies, not call playbooks.

## Outbound playbooks

Open **Broadcasts**, then **Templates**.

The heading is **Smart Outbound Templates**. The line under it is **Pick a ready-made playbook. Clara will handle the calling.**

Click a playbook to see its templates. Click **New Campaign** to start a campaign from here. See [Creating campaigns](/outbound/campaigns).

## Response templates

Open [https://agentclara.app/templates](https://agentclara.app/templates).

The heading is **Response Templates**. The line under it is **Create reusable templates for common responses and structured outputs**.

<Steps>

<Step title="Click Create New Template">
The form is **Define a reusable response template for Clara to use**.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill it in">
**Template Title**. Example: Appointment Confirmation.

**Template Content**. The form's example fills in the appointment date and time.

**Category** is **General**, **Appointment**, **Support**, **Sales**, or **Billing**.

**Target Mode** can be **Booking** or **Qualification**.

**Tags** are comma-separated.

**Status** is **Active**, **Draft**, or **Archived**.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Click **Create Template**. You should see **Template Created**.

Edit later with **Edit Template** and **Save Changes**.
</Step>

</Steps>

The same Knowledge area also has a **Learned Responses** view. See [Knowledge](/teach-clara/knowledge).
