## Get Clara running

Do these in order. You can change any of them later.

<Steps>

<Step title="Sign up">
Go to [https://agentclara.app](https://agentclara.app).

The boxes are **Email**, **First name (optional)**, and **Choose a password**. The line under them is **By continuing, you agree to our** **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy** and **Data Processing Agreement**.

When it asks for a plan, the line is **One last thing — which plan suits you? You can change it any time.** Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
</Step>

<Step title="Open Agents">
In the sidebar, open **Agents**.

The page has these tabs: **Basic**, **Personality**, **Routing**, **Automation**, **Security**, and **Launch**.

Start with **Basic** and **Personality**. [Your first agent](/getting-started/your-first-agent) walks through them.
</Step>

<Step title="Add one answer">
Open **Knowledge**.

Add a question and an answer your customers actually ask. Example: your hours.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect one way in">
Pick one:

- **Phone Numbers**, then **Get a Number**, for calls and SMS
- **Integrations**, for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or a CRM
- **Chat Widget**, to put Clara on your website

You do not need all of them on day one.
</Step>

<Step title="Test before customers do">
Open **Chat Lab** and send a question you just taught her.

If you added a phone number, open **Voice Lab** and call her.
</Step>

</Steps>

## If you run an agency

Do not use the steps above for each client. Go to [https://agentclara.app/start/agency](https://agentclara.app/start/agency), then follow [Agency setup](/agency-setup/overview).
