## Start from Broadcasts

Open **Broadcasts** in the sidebar.

Open the **Campaigns** tab.

You need an agent selected. If you have not made one, do [Your first agent](/getting-started/your-first-agent) first, and add a number in [Setting up voice](/voice-calls/setting-up-voice).

## Create a campaign

Click **New Campaign**.

You can also start from a playbook. On the **Templates** tab, the heading is **Smart Outbound Templates**. The page says **Pick a ready-made playbook. Clara will handle the calling.**

Click a playbook to open its templates, or click **New Campaign** there.

## Before you launch

Check these:

- The agent has a focus in [Clara's brief](/getting-started/your-first-agent).
- **Knowledge** has the answers she should use on the call.
- **Phone Numbers** has an outbound number. Use **Set Outbound** on that number.
- You are allowed to call those people. Calling rules are your responsibility. The product's own compliance screen is [Compliance](/outbound/compliance).

## After it is running

Stay on **Broadcasts**. **Campaigns** is the list. **Bookings** shows **Booking Health (24h)** and **Booking activity in the last 24 hours**.
