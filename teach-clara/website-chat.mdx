## Open Chat Widget

Go to [https://agentclara.app/integrations/chat-widget](https://agentclara.app/integrations/chat-widget).

The heading is **Chat Widget**. The line under it is **Customize and embed your chat widget on any website**.

## Create the widget

<Steps>

<Step title="Click Create Widget">
If you have more than one, pick the one you are editing.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect an agent">
**Agent Connection** asks **Select which agent powers this chat widget**.

The agent's personality, knowledge, and AI settings are what visitors get.

If you see **No agent connected**, the widget cannot reply. Enable the agent on the **Agents** page first. The widget shows **Live** or **Disabled**.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the look">
Open **Appearance**.

- **Widget Name**
- **Agent Display Name**
- **Primary Color** and **Button Color**
- **Position**: **Bottom Right**, **Bottom Left**, **Top Right**, or **Top Left**
- **Border Radius**
</Step>

<Step title="Set the messages">
The **Messages** section says **Widget messages take priority over agent settings**.

**Welcome Message** shows when a visitor opens the chat. The starter text is `Hi! How can I help you today?`

**Away Message** shows outside business hours if that is turned on. The starter text is `We're currently away. Leave a message and we'll get back to you!`

**Input Placeholder** starts as `Type a message...`
</Step>

<Step title="Choose who sees it">
**Audience** has three groups:

- **Visitors** — not logged in, and no conversation yet
- **Leads** — someone who starts a chat or replies to an outbound message
- **Users** — someone who has signed up or logged in
</Step>

<Step title="Choose when it opens">
**Triggers** includes **Enabled** (the widget is visible), **Exit Intent**, **Mobile Enabled**, **Auto-open Delay (seconds)**, and **Scroll Trigger (%)**.

Leave a delay empty to disable it.
</Step>

<Step title="Embed it">
Open **Embed** and copy the code. You should see **Embed code copied!**

Paste that code on your website.
</Step>

</Steps>

Use **Preview** on the page before you publish the site.

Chats from the widget show in the **Inbox** under **Chat Widget**.
