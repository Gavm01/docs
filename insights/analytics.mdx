## Open Analytics

In the sidebar, open **Analytics**, or go to [https://agentclara.app/reports-v2](https://agentclara.app/reports-v2).

The heading is **Analytics**. The line under it is **Is Clara making you money? Live business insights.**

If you see **Access Denied**, the line under it is **You don't have permission to view Analytics for this workspace. Please contact your workspace administrator to request access.** It also says **If you believe this is an error, please contact support.**

## The tabs

- **Overview**
- **Performance**
- **Conversations**
- **Customer satisfaction**
- **Monitors**, if you can view monitors

The export button is labeled **Export all tabs to PDF**. Hover it and it says **Export all tabs to PDF via your browser's print dialog**.

Older report links, such as customer satisfaction on its own, send you back to this page.
