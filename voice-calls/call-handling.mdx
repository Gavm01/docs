## Open call routing

In the sidebar, open **Agents**.

Open the **Routing** tab, or go to [https://agentclara.app/agent?tab=routing](https://agentclara.app/agent?tab=routing).

The old **Call routing** address sends you to this same tab.

## What this tab is for

**Call Routing** is where you set how calls are handled.

**Voicemail Handling** is on this tab. **When voicemail is detected**, you pick one action:

- **Hang up immediately**
- **Leave a voicemail message**
- **Hang up and follow outbound retry logic**

If you leave a message, you can write the **Voicemail message**. See [Voicemail](/voice-calls/voicemail).

## What Clara needs before she can take a call

1. A number on [Phone Numbers](/voice-calls/setting-up-voice).
2. An agent with **Basic** and **Personality** filled in. See [Your first agent](/getting-started/your-first-agent).
3. Answers in **Knowledge**, so she is not guessing.

Then open **Voice Lab** and call her yourself.

## Bookings on a call

Clara can book, change, or cancel an appointment on an inbound call. Those appointments show on [Bookings](/customers/bookings).

Billing treats a successful inbound booking call as a billable resolution. A transfer to a person, a hang-up before she finishes, spam, a failed call, or a duplicate within an hour is not billed. See [Billing](/account/billing).
