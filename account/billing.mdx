## Open billing

Go to [https://agentclara.app/billing](https://agentclara.app/billing).

The heading is **Billing Overview**.

You can also open **Settings**, then **Billing & Usage**.

This page is for your own subscription. Agency commission and client plans are in [Agency billing](/agency-setup/billing).

## What you see

- **Current Plan**
- **Trial**, if you are in one
- **Next Billing Date**
- **Last Invoice**

If you have free credit, the page shows **Your £100 free credit** and the date it ends. You are not charged the subscription fee until then.

**Payment Failed – Action Required** means you need to click **Update Payment Method**.

## What gets billed

**What Counts as a Billable Resolution** includes:

- An inbound call where Clara answers the question and the call ends successfully
- An inbound call where Clara books, changes, or cancels an appointment
- A chat where Clara resolves the issue
- An outbound call where Clara reaches the customer and completes a follow-up or upsell
- A meaningful interaction the AI handles end to end, with no transfer to a person

**Non-Billable Cases** are:

- A transfer to a human, with no AI resolution
- A timeout, or the customer hangs up before resolution
- Spam, robocalls, and test interactions
- Technical errors and failed calls
- A duplicate about the same issue within 1 hour

The page also says there is a maximum of 1 charge per resolved case.

## Invoices

**Invoices & Payments** says billing is automatic via Stripe. Invoices are generated and emailed monthly.

The history columns include **Date**, **Period Start**, **Period End**, **Plan Fee (£)**, **Interactions**, **Overage (£)**, **Wallet Top-ups (£)**, **Total (£)**, and **Status**.

Click **Download Invoices** for a file. Click **Add Payment Method** to add a card. That opens a secure payment page.

If you cancelled, **Resubscribe** is on this page.

**Resolution Log** lists each AI resolution and why it was charged or free.
